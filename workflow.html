Technical Specification Notes

Request Intake

All channels (email, WhatsApp, SMS, portal, walk-in) converge into a single intake layer. Each submission generates a standardized request object with: requester identity, channel source, timestamp, raw content. A unique ticket ID (REQ-YYYY-XXXX) is assigned immediately.

Taxonomic Categorization

A keyword-scoring engine analyzes the subject and description against category dictionaries (HR, IT, Payroll, Operations). Multi-word matches receive higher weight. A confidence score (0–100%) is computed. Requests below a 40% confidence threshold are flagged for manual review.

Dynamic Routing

Category determines the department queue. Each department has a designated owner (team or individual). Notifications are dispatched to both the assignee and the requester. Round-robin or workload-based assignment can be layered in at scale.

Escalation Protocol

SLA timers are set by priority: P1 (4h), P2 (8h), P3 (24h), P4 (48h). When breached, the ticket auto-escalates to P1, alerts the department head, and flags in the dashboard. A second escalation to senior leadership triggers at 2× the original SLA window.

State Lifecycle

Tickets move through four states: Open → In Progress → Resolved → Closed. Each transition is logged with a timestamp and actor. Reopening a resolved ticket reverts it to In Progress with a new SLA window. Closed tickets are archived and indexed for analytics.

Resolution & Archival

Upon resolution, the requester receives a summary notification. Satisfaction can be captured (thumbs up/down or NPS). Unconfirmed resolutions auto-close after 48 hours. All closed tickets feed into the analytics layer for trend analysis, SLA compliance, and workload insights.
